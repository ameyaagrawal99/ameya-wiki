## Who He Is

Ameya Agrawal is a **strategy leader, social entrepreneur, author, and builder** based in Pune, India. He sits at an uncommon intersection: institutional strategy (Strategy Manager / Chief of Staff at MIT World Peace University), social impact at scale (125,000+ people through MGSS, 25,000+ through SkillSlate), active intellectual output (3 Forbes India articles, blog, Medium, book), and hands-on software building (10 GitHub repos).

He is an IIM Kozhikode Gold Medalist (2024) and a BE in Electronics & Telecom with Distinction (MIT COE Pune, 2016). He pursued his MBA alongside his wife Vrushali Agrawal, who was in the same cohort. A self-described "shy soul" who loves theatrical performance, he began writing at school age and holds a national record as the youngest engineering student to write a self-motivation book — *A Leap Within*, published at age 20–21.

His decision to decline Harvard and LSE in 2021 in favour of building SkillSlate and leading MGSS is the defining biographical moment — validated by two Presidential National Awards.
