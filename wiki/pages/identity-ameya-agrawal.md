# Ameya Agrawal — Identity & Profile

## Tagline

> "I dropped out of Harvard to make a change. The inclusive way."

## Self-Description

*"Social impact trailblazer, Scribbling Wordsmith, MBA-bound dream-chaser, empathetic leader and aspiring global change-maker."*[^1]

*"Overthinker, Author of A Leap Within, Speaker, National Record Holder. In search of self."*[^2]

*"A person who don't know to settle for less."*[^3]

## Profile Summary

IIM Kozhikode Gold Medalist with **8 years of experience** in program management, change management, and organizational transformation. Led projects to win two National Awards from the President of India. Published author, national record holder, and public speaker featured in Forbes, Financial Express, Poets & Quants, and Business Standard; published in Emerald (DLO Journal).[^4]

## Personal Background

- **Hometown:** Pune / Nashik, Maharashtra
- **Personality:** Self-described "shy soul" who loves theatrical performance[^1]
- **Partner:** Married to **Vrushali Agrawal** — pursued MBA together at IIM Kozhikode in the same cohort[^1]
- **Writing habit:** 4,000+ hours of reflective content through his "Lyrics of Success" initiative; 1,800+ quotes authored[^1]
- **Book written at age 20–21** — holds national record as youngest engineering student to write a self-motivation book[^3]
