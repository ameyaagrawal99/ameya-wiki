# Mahatma Gandhi Seva Sangh (MGSS)

**Founded:** 1994, Parbhani, Maharashtra  
**Ameya's Role:** Head of Strategy and Business Development (2021–2023)  
**Award:** Best NGO in India 2022 — Department of Disabilities, Government of India  
**See also:** [[skillslate-foundation]] — Ameya's other flagship social initiative

## What MGSS Does

MGSS is a nationally recognized NGO focused on disability rehabilitation and empowerment of persons with disabilities (PWDs / Divyangjan). It operates **10 District Disability Rehabilitation Centres** across Maharashtra and Goa, with additional work in Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Punjab, Bihar, and West Bengal.[^1]

**Services:**
- Healthcare and social services
- Education and rehabilitation
- Assistive device distribution
- Socio-economic mapping
- Government policy advocacy
- Authorized ALIMCO dealer (assistive devices)

## The Akola Pattern

Ameya led **India's first large-scale socio-economic mapping of persons with disabilities** in Akola district, Maharashtra — covering **1.5 million participants**. This initiative, known as the "Akola Pattern":

- Changed disability policy implementation across India
- Earned a **National Award from the President of India**
- Led to state-level implementation across Maharashtra
- Established **11 District Disability Rehabilitation Centers** benefiting 100,000+ individuals[^3]
