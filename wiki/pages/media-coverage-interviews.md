## Poets & Quants — PGP-BL Class of 2024 Profile

**Published:** November 3, 2023 | [Read full profile ↗](https://poetsandquants.com/2023/11/03/meet-the-pgp-bl-class-of-2024-ameya-agrawal-iim-kozhikode/)

Key details shared:
- Pursuing MBA alongside his wife **Vrushali Agrawal** (same cohort at IIM KO)[^1]
- Self-described as a "shy soul" who loves theatrical performance[^1]
- Post-MBA vision: management consulting in social impact; collaborating with World Bank and UN[^1]
- Favorite course: Organizational Behavior (Prof. Roopak Gupta)[^1]
- Launched **#IIMKaleidoscope** — student interview series documenting peers' resilience[^1]
- Self-description: *"Social impact trailblazer, Scribbling Wordsmith, MBA-bound dream-chaser, empathetic leader and aspiring global change-maker"*[^1]
