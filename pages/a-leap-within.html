A Leap Within

Overview

A Leap Within is Ameya Agrawal's debut self-help book, published by Kalpaz Publication, New Delhi in 2015. Written during his 2nd and 3rd year of engineering, it was completed over approximately 15 months. Available on Amazon and Flipkart.1

He holds a national record as the youngest engineering student to write a self-motivation book — published at age 20–21.2

"This book is By the youngster, to the youngsters and for the youngsters."1

Structure

The book is divided into 7 sections covering:1
1. Life fundamentals
2. Understanding existence
3. Self-exploration
4. Love
5. Societal connection
6. Personality development
7. Practical lessons

Includes personal stories, experiences, and motivational one-liners. Sections can be read in any order without losing coherence.1

Reception

  • 2,000+ copies sold3
  • 45 ratings on Goodreads4
  • Appreciated by Radhakrishnan Pillai (author of Corporate Chanakya)1
  • Readers noted surprise at a 20-year-old tackling complex psychological topics1
  • Led to speaking invitations at educational institutions across India1

Key Quotes from the Book

  • "What is the best thing about people doubting your potential? The pleasure of proving them wrong."4
  • "When you are worried about thinking why you can't, you are losing reasons why you should."4
  • "Entrepreneurship is a responsibility rather than a profession."4
  • "The reason some people put on a mask is not in their blood but it is in their fear that we judge them too soon."4
  • "Boss may be one, but leader can be anyone!"4
  • "Hard work doesn't guarantee success, dedication and persistence do."2

Writing Motivation

"Writing that one page at a time kept me inspiring."1

Writing started during school as a way to document feelings and thoughts. His mother preserved a 100-page early manuscript. Engineering was difficult; writing became his hope through it.1
