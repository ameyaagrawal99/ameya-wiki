Overview

Ameya's blog is a personal essay collection exploring the intersection of leadership, organizational psychology, technology, and AI's effect on work and education. Posts use structured numerical frameworks ("5 Warning Signs", "7 Lessons") to deliver actionable insights.

Recurring Themes

Writing Style

  • Numbered listicles with conceptual framing ("5 signs", "7 truths")
  • Essay-length deep dives, not short-form
  • Psychological and behavioral science lens applied to workplace and leadership topics
