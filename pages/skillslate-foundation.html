SkillSlate Foundation

Website: skillslate.org
YouTube: youtube.com/@SkillSlate
Founder: Ameya Agrawal
Active period: April 2020 – March 2021 (as startup); ongoing as foundation
See also: MGSS — Ameya's other flagship social initiative

Mission & Vision

Mission: "Help every individual find the right course or skill trainer, at the right time and a price they can afford."

Vision: Make education, skills, and opportunities accessible to all.

Guiding philosophy: "Give a man a fish and you feed him for a day; teach a man to fish and you feed him for a lifetime."1

What It Does

SkillSlate connects learners with certified, standardized courses and trainers. It also offers branding and website design services for startups and tech companies. Platform includes a blog, community, and learning management features.1

Impact

  • 25,000+ individuals trained across India and 4 countries2
  • 1,000+ participants per event (workshops, conferences, seminars)2
  • 10+ university and college partnerships supporting NAAC accreditation2
  • 4.7/5 average rating based on 1,500+ Trustpilot and Google Reviews2
  • 10,000+ person community via multi-channel digital platforms2

Events

  • 2-Day IPR Workshop (with Central University of Haryana)1
  • International Women's Day Conference (March 2022)1
  • Seminars with Vice-Chancellors, Directors, Celebrities, and Entrepreneurs2

Context

SkillSlate was one of the key reasons Ameya declined Harvard and LSE in 2021 — choosing grassroots impact in India over institutional prestige. The GTM strategy, community building, and stakeholder engagement were all personally led.2
